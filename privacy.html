CardFlipPro Privacy

CardFlipPro stores your watchlist in your browser using local storage. We do not currently require an account.

When you use card search, your request may be sent to the public Pokémon TCG API. When you use the scanner, camera video is processed in your browser for OCR and is not intentionally uploaded by CardFlipPro.

Third-party links may have their own privacy policies. CardFlipPro does not currently process payments or collect payment card information.

This page is informational and may be updated as the service grows.
